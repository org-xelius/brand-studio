# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release.

- Ten authored social arrangements with schematic pickers that redraw the main canvas.
- Six bundled SIL OFL font pairings with per-role typography overrides.
- Independent background, subject and product layers with literal opacity.
- Independent CTA styling, including true poster-width actions.
- Four company poster colours with contrast-guarded text.
- Campaign planning, company defaults and campaign look inheritance for new posts.
- Review-bound native PNG export across four output formats.
- Frozen rendering generations so older saved designs keep their original appearance.
